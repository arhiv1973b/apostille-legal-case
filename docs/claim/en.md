I. SUBJECT OF DISPUTE
The Plaintiff files this lawsuit against the State of the Republic of Moldova, represented by the Ministry of Finance as the primary guarantor of the state’s international financial obligations, for compensation for material and moral damage resulting from:
 * Systematic violation of the right to a fair trial (Art. 6 ECHR).
 * Failure to investigate torture and cruel treatment allegations dating back to the 1998 nexus (Art. 3 ECHR).
 * Denial of an effective remedy (Art. 13 ECHR).
 * Breach of International Treaty Obligations: Specifically regarding the arbitrary invalidation of e-apostilles within the domestic jurisdiction while asserting their validity abroad, creating a state-level liability outside the administrative competence of the Ministry of Justice.
II. JURISDICTIONAL JUSTIFICATION FOR THE DEFENDANT
The Ministry of Finance is identified as the proper Defendant based on the following:
 * Hague Convention (Art. 5): The e-apostille procedure, by its nature and international standing, is governed by international law. The state's assertion that an e-apostille is "invalid on the territory of Moldova but valid outside" constitutes a violation of the treaty's integrity. Such a conflict between domestic practice and international commitment is a matter of State Responsibility, which involves the state treasury.
 * Rome Statute (Art. 5, 7, 17, 29, 32): Given the systemic nature of the violations (torture and denial of rehabilitation), the matter falls under the scope of international crimes and state failure (Art. 17 - Inability or Unwillingness of the State). Under Art. 29, these claims are not subject to statutes of limitation.
 * Financial Guarantee: Only a central financial institution, such as the Ministry of Finance in coordination with the National Bank of Moldova, can act as the guarantor for damages arising from the non-observance of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR) and other international treaties. Because the issue of e-apostille validity has been placed outside the scope of domestic legislation by the state's own contradictory declarations, it falls under the direct financial liability of the State Treasury.
III. FACTS OF THE CASE
1. Timeline of Events (1997–2026)
For 29 years, the Plaintiff has been subjected to a "Continuum of Violations," starting with the crystallization of torture in 1998. This judicial persecution includes the mass issuance of 90 apostilles (2021–2023) which have been effectively neutralized by domestic authorities, rendering the legalization process a "procedural fraud."
2. Apostille and Procedural Violations
The Ministry of Justice has demonstrated an inability to manage the integrity of the apostille registry:
 * 13 apostilles (14.4%) feature "unclear signatures."
 * Systemic signatures by officials (Guzun, Miron, Iordan) without substantive verification of the underlying court decisions.
 * This mass "automatic legalization" without domestic recognition violates the spirit of the 1961 Hague Convention.
