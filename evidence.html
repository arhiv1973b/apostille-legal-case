Constitutional Court — Refusal on Language Grounds
Application to Moldova Constitutional Court denied on language grounds. The Vienna Convention Art. 85 establishes equal authenticity of multiple official languages. Refusal on language grounds violates ECHR Art. 6 (fair trial) and Art. 13 (effective remedy). This denial created an absolute bar to constitutional remedy, compounding all prior apostille violations.
