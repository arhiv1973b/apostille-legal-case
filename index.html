📨 Diplomatic Notification

Re: Civil Case — Republic of Moldova — Apostille Violations

This notification is submitted pursuant to the Hague Convention (1961), Vienna Convention Art. 53 & 64 (jus cogens), and ECHR Art. 3, 6, 13.

The Republic of Moldova has systematically processed 90 apostilles (1997–2026) in violation of authentication standards. 14.4% bear unclear signatures. Constitutional remedies were denied (2009) on procedural grounds in violation of the right to a fair trial.

The claimant Macheret Alexei Artur requests diplomatic intervention and recognition of jus cogens violations under international law. Claim: €500,000.
